# Privacy Policy — Adventure Day

**Last updated: 1 September 2026**

Adventure Day is a game for young children. It is designed so that there is
nothing to collect, and nothing to send anywhere.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Adventure Day does not collect, store, transmit or share any
personal information whatsoever.

We do not ask for a name, an email address, an age, a photograph, a location,
or any other personal detail. There is no sign-up, no account, and no login.

## Internet access

Adventure Day **has no internet permission**. The app cannot connect to the
internet at all, even if it wanted to. It works entirely offline, on the
device, forever.

You can verify this yourself: the app requests no Android permissions of any
kind.

## Advertising and analytics

There are **no adverts**, **no analytics**, **no tracking**, and **no
third-party software development kits** of any sort in Adventure Day.

Nothing about how the game is played is measured or reported to anybody.

## Purchases

There are **no in-app purchases** and nothing to buy. The game is complete as
downloaded.

## Saved data

The game saves your child's progress — the things she has found, what she has
built, her drawings — to a single file in the app's own private storage on the
device. That file never leaves the device and cannot be read by us or by
anybody else.

Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Children

Adventure Day is intended for children and complies with the Google Play
Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the
UK/EU GDPR provisions on children's data — trivially so, because no data is
collected from anybody, of any age.

## Photographs used in the game

The photographs of wildlife, meadows and dinosaur models shown inside the game
are stock photographs licensed from Pexels under the Pexels License, and are
bundled with the app. They are listed in the app's credits. The photographs of
the two dogs are the developer's own.

The game's "camera" takes pictures of drawings inside the game only. It does
**not** use your device's camera. The app has no camera permission.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same
address, with a new date at the top.
